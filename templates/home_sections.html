About StudentSense

About the platform

StudentSense gives students a private place to reflect on their routines, workload, sleep, and recovery. It turns those reflections into supportive information that can help students decide when to keep tracking, reach out, or book time with a counselor.

Getting started

How to use it

Use the navigation at the top of the page to move between these features. Each page has guidance to help you take the next step.

Check-ins

Open Check-in → New check-in to answer the short 1–5 questionnaire about your recent school experience. Review your support result and recommendations when you submit, then use My progress to compare earlier check-ins.

Messages

Open Support → Messages to see your counselor conversations in one place. Select a conversation to read replies, send a message, or use Urgent help needed when you need prompt assistance.

Appointments

Open Appointments → Book an appointment to choose a counselor and an open time slot. Your confirmed appointments are available under My appointments, where you can review or cancel an upcoming booking.

Teacher contacts

Open Support → Teacher contacts to view approved teacher contacts when you would prefer to speak with a trusted teacher. Phone numbers are shown for reference, and Messages remains available for counselor support.
