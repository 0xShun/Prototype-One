About StudentSense

A simple platform for student stress check-ins.

StudentSense helps students record routine habits, get an immediate stress snapshot, and look back at changes over time. Counselors can use the dashboard to spot students who may need support.

For studentsCheck in and track progress
For counselorsReview at-risk students
Current versionPlatform-ready without the final model

Why it exists

The goal is to make stress check-ins easy, understandable, and useful without making the experience feel heavy.

What it stores

Each intake becomes a result entry so students can review their own history and counselors can see trends.

What comes next

The prediction layer can later be replaced with a trained model without changing the overall workflow.

References

These are the studies used to shape the intake questions and their supporting guidance.

  • Credé, M., & Kuncel, N. R. (2008). Study habits, skills, and attitudes: The third pillar supporting collegiate academic performance.
  • Pascoe, M. C., Hetrick, S. E., & Parker, A. G. (2020). The impact of stress on students in secondary school and higher education.
  • Pilcher, J. J., & Huffcutt, A. I. (1996). Effects of sleep deprivation on performance: A meta-analysis.
  • Schuch, F. B., et al. (2018). Physical activity and incident depression: A meta-analysis of prospective cohort studies.
  • Hunt, M. G., Marx, R., Lipson, C., & Young, J. (2018). No more FOMO: Limiting social media decreases loneliness and depression.
